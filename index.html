To You, Kath 🤍

A letter written with genuine feelings and respect

To you, Kath,

I just want you to know that my feelings for you are genuine. But I understand that we're both not ready for a relationship right now, and I respect that.

I don't want to rush you, and I don't want to enter a relationship when I'm still working on myself and my financial stability. I want to become a better version of myself, not just for someone else, but for my own future, too.

Maybe we met at the wrong time, or maybe we still have a lot of growing to do. But sometimes, I wonder what if, in another universe, we meet again when we're both ready to love, ready to commit, and ready to choose each other without fear or hesitation.

I hope that in another universe, we meet again, but this time, the timing is right for both of us.

Until then, I'll respect your pace and continue working on myself. No pressure, no promises needed. I just hope life treats us well, wherever it takes us.

Take care, Kath. 🤍

— Mark
